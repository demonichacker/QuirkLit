About QuirkLit

QuirkLit is on the hunt for storytellers with a passion for the extraordinary. If you’re a novelist or long story writer with compelling characters, vivid worlds, and unforgettable plots, we want to hear from you. Whether your stories are grounded in reality or soar into the fantastical, QuirkLit is dedicated to helping you share your creative vision with the world.
Your voice matters—let’s bring your story to life.

Why Choose QuirkLit?

We believe in nurturing talent and providing opportunities for writers to flourish. With a dedicated team of editors, marketers, and publishers, QuirkLit ensures that your journey from manuscript to publication is seamless and rewarding.

  • Personalized Feedback: Get constructive feedback on your work.
  • Wide Reach: Our network ensures your stories reach a global audience.

How It Works

To sign a contract with us and take your writing career to the next level, here’s what you need to do:

  1. Submit a writing sample of at least 5000 words.
  2. Our team will review your submission and provide feedback.
  3. If your work meets our standards, we’ll invite you to sign a contract and begin your journey with QuirkLit!

Submission Rules

  1. Only English books are qualified.
  2. The erotic content in a book cannot exceed 10%.
  3. Fanfiction is not accepted.
  4. Books must be originally written by authors, and no plagiarism is allowed.
  5. The book must be a novel; any other form of writing will not be accepted.
